About the usage counter

The live site contacts CountAPI to show the shared total and add one after each completed GIF. No videos, GIFs or filenames are sent. The service receives normal connection information such as your IP address. No cookies or personal identifiers are added by this app. This public counter is approximate; blocked requests and manual changes can affect it.
